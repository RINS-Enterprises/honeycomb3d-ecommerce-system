MakerWorld to Drive

Save a MakerWorld model into our shared Drive folder in one go, from a phone, tablet or computer.

  • The designer's photos and the community print photos from the comments.
  • Filament grams and print time from the print profile, with the suggested price worked out from our costs.
  • Your own print photos, and AI versions of any photo (new background, colour or finish).

First time, on any device

  1. Open the app link the owner sent you, signed in with the Google account the owner shared the folder with.
  2. Google asks for permission once. Tap Review permissions, choose your account, then Advanced → Go to MakerWorld to Drive (unsafe) → Allow. The warning appears because the app is ours, not a published one.
  3. At the top you should see Signed in as your address and a Shared folder link. The photos you save are added under your name.

Android phone or tablet

  1. Open the app link in Chrome and tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen. From then on, open it like any app.
  2. In the MakerWorld app or website, open the model and tap Share → Copy link.
  3. Open MakerWorld to Drive, paste the link and tap Get model.
  4. Check what it found:
    • the print profile fills in grams, hours and the price; pick another profile if you print it differently;
    • untick photos you don't want;
    • tap Add photos… for your own print photos;
    • tick the licence box only if we bought the commercial licence or the licence allows selling.
  5. Tap Save to Drive.

If it says "MakerWorld did not let Google's server read this page"

MakerWorld sometimes blocks the automatic read. Tap Fill in by hand, then:

  • in MakerWorld, long-press each photo you want and tap Download image;
  • back in the app, choose Designer photos or Community prints next to Add photos… and pick the downloaded photos;
  • type the grams and print time from the model's print profile and tap Save to Drive.

Windows computer

The paste-a-link page works the same in Chrome or Edge. The one-click bookmark is better on a computer, because it reads the page in your own browser and also catches the community photos and print profiles that only load while you scroll.

Add the bookmark once

  1. Show the bookmarks bar with Ctrl+Shift+B.
  2. Right-click the bar → Add page (Chrome) or Add favourite (Edge).
  3. Name it Save to HoneyComb Drive and paste the bookmark line the owner sent you (it starts with javascript:) as the URL.

Use it

  1. On a MakerWorld model page, scroll through the photos, open the print profile list and scroll the comments and prints. Press "load more" for more community photos.
  2. Click Save to HoneyComb Drive. A new tab opens with everything it found. If nothing opens, allow pop-ups for makerworld.com.
  3. Check the details and photos as on Android, then click Save to Drive.

AI versions

Straight after saving, or later from Recent imports → AI versions on the start page.

  1. Pick up to 6 photos per run.
  2. Pick a preset or write your own instructions. For a colour, change "red" or "gold" in the text.

Example row, priced with the app's example cost settings. Real prices use the owner's saved settings: grams × (1 + waste) × filament cost per kg + hours × machine cost + labour + packaging, plus the markup, rounded up.

House rules

  • Tick the licence box only when we really have the commercial licence or the licence allows selling. Unticked rows are marked "NO - check before listing".
  • Community photos belong to the people who posted them. Use them as reference and for AI edits, not as our product photos.
  • Every product on the website needs at least one real photo of our own print. AI images go up labelled, for example "Colour preview".

Problems

"You don't have access to the shared folder"
The folder isn't shared with the account you're signed in with. Ask the owner to share MakerWorld imports with that address as Editor.
An error right after opening, or the wrong account shows
You're signed in to more than one Google account in this browser. Open the app in a window signed in only to the shared account, for example an incognito window or your own Chrome profile.
Wrong title, grams or photos
Fix them on the page before saving; everything is editable. Tell the owner which model it was so the reader can be improved.
"Only the owner can save the pricing settings"
Prices use shared settings. You can try other numbers for one model, but only the owner saves them for everyone.
