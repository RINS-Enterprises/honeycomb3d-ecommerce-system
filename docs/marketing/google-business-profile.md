# Google Business Profile: a new listing you control

Goal: a **new** HoneyComb 3D profile on Google Search and Maps, owned by your own Google account, showing **071 059 9815** and **honeycomb3d.store**, with **no public home address**.

Time: about 30 minutes to set up, plus verification (Google decides how; it can take a few days).

Nothing has been created. You do these steps yourself.

---

## Before you start: is HoneyComb 3D eligible?

Google only allows a profile for a business that **meets customers in person**: either at a shop, or by going to them (a "service-area business"). A purely online shop that only posts parcels is not allowed, and the listing can be suspended.

You qualify as a service-area business if you **hand over orders in person** in the Kegalle area (delivering, or meeting customers) during your stated hours. Only list the areas where you really do that.

If you only ever send parcels by courier, **don't create the profile yet**. Rely on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and the website instead, and come back to this when you start meeting customers locally. Tell Claude which is true; it changes the steps below.

## Step by step

### 1. Use the right Google account
Sign in with the **new email** from `launch-kit.md` (the one only you control). Turn on 2-step verification.

### 2. Create the profile
1. Go to **business.google.com** → **Manage now** / **Add business**.
2. **Business name:** `HoneyComb 3D`. Exactly the name you use everywhere. Don't add keywords such as "Kegalle" or "3D printing" to the name: Google's rules forbid it and can suspend the listing.
3. If Google shows "**This business might already exist**" and lists an older HoneyComb listing, **don't select it**. Choose the option to continue and create a new one (wording like "None of these" or "I don't see my business").
4. **Business category** (start typing and pick from Google's list):
   - Primary: **3D printing service**
   - If the exact name isn't offered, pick the closest one Google suggests for 3D printing. Don't invent one.
   - You can add more categories after setup (step 4).
5. **"Do you want to add a location customers can visit, like a store or office?"** → **No**.
6. **Service areas:** add the places where you really meet customers or hand over orders, e.g. **Kegalle** and nearby towns you go to. Google allows up to 20 areas; keep it to where you can actually reach in person.
7. **Contact details:**
   - Phone: **071 059 9815**
   - Website: **https://honeycomb3d.store**
8. **Updates and recommendations:** your choice.
9. **Mailing address for verification:** Google asks for your real address **only to verify you**. Because you chose "No" in step 5, it **is not shown publicly**. Use your own address in Kegalle.

### 3. Verify
Google picks the method. Common ones in 2026:
- **Video verification:** a short, unbroken phone video. Plan it before you press record:
  1. Outside: a street sign or landmark, then your building, so Google can see you're in Kegalle.
  2. Inside: your 3D printer running and finished prints (shows the business is real).
  3. Proof you run it: e.g. your HoneyComb business cards or packaging if you have them, or open the WhatsApp Business app showing the HoneyComb 3D profile on 071 059 9815.
- **Phone or SMS code** to 071 059 9815.
- **Postcard** to your address (can take 2+ weeks).

The profile isn't public until verification is approved.

### 4. Fill in the profile

**Additional categories** (only ones that describe what you do; pick from Google's list):
- Gift shop
- Hobby store

**Hours:** the hours you actually answer WhatsApp and hand over orders. If they change a lot, keep it simple, e.g. Mon–Sat 9 am – 7 pm. Don't list 24 hours.

**Description** (Google allows 750 characters; no links, phone numbers or offers in it):

> HoneyComb 3D makes 3D-printed anime, film and comic figures, keychains, props and desk accessories in Kegalle, Sri Lanka. Every piece is printed to order: browse the designs at our website, send your choice on WhatsApp, and we confirm the size, colour, finish, price and delivery with you before we print. We also take custom requests: share a model link or describe your idea and we'll tell you what's possible.

(about 420 characters)

**Opening date:** leave blank unless you know the real date you started under your own name.

**Attributes:** tick only what's true (e.g. "Online appointments" only if you offer them).

### 6. Products (optional, good for search)
In the profile, **Edit products → Add product** for your main designs. For each: the photo, the **exact name and price from honeycomb3d.store**, a short description, and the button **"Order online" → the product's page link** (e.g. `https://honeycomb3d.store/products/kurama-figure/`; copy the address from your browser). Start with Kurama Figure (LKR 1,500), Night Fury Keychain (LKR 500), Minato Kunai (LKR 1,000) and Urban Spider-Man (LKR 1,750). If a price changes on the site, change it here too.

### 7. Reviews
Get your review link: profile → **Ask for reviews** (or "Get more reviews") → copy the link. Then follow `reviews.md`. Reply to every review, good or bad, politely and briefly.

## The old Kandy listing

There's an older HoneyComb listing in Menikhinna, Kandy, controlled by your former partner. You don't need to contact them. Your choices, from simplest:

**A. Leave it, make yours clearly different (recommended to start).**
Your new profile has its own area (Kegalle), phone (071 059 9815) and website (honeycomb3d.store). Point all your customers to the new one: put the new Maps link on the website, Instagram and WhatsApp. Over time, your reviews and activity build up on the new one.

**B. Suggest an edit, only for things that are untrue.**
On Google Maps, open the old listing → **Suggest an edit**:
- **"Change name or other details"**: only if it shows facts that are wrong, e.g. your personal number or your website.
- **"Close or remove" → "Permanently closed"**: **only if that business is no longer operating at that place.** Don't mark it closed if your former partner still runs it; that's a false report and can backfire on your own listing.

Edits are anonymous to the listing owner; Google checks them and decides.

**C. Report it, for clear rule breaks.**
- If it uses **your own photos** (you took them) or **your new phone number**: on the listing, tap the photo → **Report** (or the ⋮ menu → **Report a problem**), and say what's yours.
- For photos or content you own, Google also has a legal removal form: search "Google legal removal request Maps". You'll need to show the photos are yours (e.g. the original files with dates).

**Don't:**
- Use **"Request ownership"** / "Claim this business": Google emails the current owner (your former partner) about it.
- Post reviews on the old listing, or ask friends to.
- Copy anything from the old listing into the new one.

If you're unsure which applies, tell Claude what the old listing currently shows (a screenshot is fine), and it'll suggest the exact option.
