For **new accounts you create and control**. Don't log in to, link to, tag or mention any old HoneyComb account, and don't copy posts or photos from them.

Nothing here has been created or posted. You do the sign-ups; Claude prepares the words and pictures.

1. **A new email you control**, used only for these accounts. A free Gmail is fine. Pick something close to the handle you choose (section 1). Turn on 2-step verification.

4. **Write down** every login in a password manager or a private notebook. Don't share logins with anyone, and use 2-step verification on every account.

### Highlight covers to make

Story highlights sit under the bio. Each needs a cover: a **1080 × 1920** image, studio black background, a simple **honey (`#FBA31B`) line icon** in the centre (about 400 px wide), no text. Make them all the same style.

| Highlight name | Icon idea | What goes in it |
|---|---|---|
| How to order | chat bubble | The 4 "how to order" slides as stories |
| Figures | small statue / star | Story shares of every figure post |
| Keychains | key | Keychain posts |
| Props | kunai / shuriken | Kunai and shuriken posts |
| Custom | plus sign `+` | "Bring your own idea" stories and custom work (with the customer's OK) |
| Printing | printer / layers | Time-lapses and behind-the-scenes |
| Reviews | heart / speech mark | **Only real customer messages and photos, shared with their permission.** Create this highlight when the first real one arrives (see `reviews.md`). |

Highlight covers are a good first Canva job. Say **"make the Canva templates"** and Claude will create them in your Canva account. Until you say so, nothing is made.
