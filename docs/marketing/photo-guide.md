# Home-studio photo guide (under LKR 3,000)

Builds on `docs/pm/NEXT-PHASE.md` §1. Consistent photos make the shop look premium more than any website change. One afternoon of setup, then about **5 minutes per product**.

## 1. Shopping list

Prices are rough guides for a bookshop or stationery shop in Kegalle in 2026. **Check before you buy**; you may already have some of these.

| Item | Use | Rough cost (LKR) |
|---|---|---|
| 2 × large sheets of **mid-grey or warm beige** card (A2 or bigger, matte, not glossy) | Everyday background ("sweep") | 300–500 |
| 1 × **black** background: black velvet or felt cloth (about 1 m), or matte black card | Hero shots on black | 400–800 |
| 1 × **white foam board** or thick white card (A3+) | Fill light (bounces window light into shadows) | 300–500 |
| 1 × sheet of **baking paper** or tracing paper | Softens the window light on sunny days | 100–200 |
| Masking tape + 4 bulldog clips | Hold the sweep and cloth | 200–300 |
| **Lint roller** (or wide sticky tape) | Removes dust from black velvet and prints | 200–400 |
| Phone stand or **mini tripod with phone clamp** (optional: a stack of books works) | Same height and angle every time | 0–1,000 |
| **Total** | | **about 1,500–3,000** |

No lights to buy: you use one window.

## 2. Set up once (same place every time)

```
          WINDOW (daylight, no direct sun)
             ║  ← baking paper over it if sunny
             ║
  [ product on sweep ]   [ white card ]  ← fill, on the side opposite the window
         ▲
       phone (on stand, at the product's height, 50–80 cm away)
```

1. **Table next to a window.** Light comes from the **side**, never from behind you and never direct sun on the product.
2. **Sweep:** tape the top of the card to the wall or a box behind the table, and let it curve down onto the table in a smooth "U". No fold or corner line behind the product.
3. **Product** about 20–30 cm in front of the curve, so the background goes soft.
4. **White card** on the side away from the window, angled toward the product, to lift the shadows.
5. **Phone** on the stand at the product's height, straight in front, about 50–80 cm away.
6. **Same window, same time of day** for every session (late morning or mid-afternoon is usually softest). This is what makes all the photos match.
7. Mark the sweep, product and phone positions with small bits of masking tape on the table so you can set up the same way next time.

## 3. Phone settings (do once)

- **Lens:** use **1×**, or **2×/3×** if your phone has a real telephoto lens. Never the 0.5× wide lens (it distorts figures). Don't pinch-zoom past the lens marks (digital zoom = blurry).
- **Turn off:** beauty mode, "AI scene optimiser", filters, portrait/blur mode, flash.
- **Turn on:** grid lines (to keep things straight), highest resolution, 3-second **timer** (stops shake when you tap).
- **Aspect ratio:** 4:3 (most detail). You'll crop to square for the website and 4:5 for Instagram later.
- **Focus and exposure:** tap on the product and **hold** until it says "AE/AF lock". Then slide the sun icon **down a little** (about −0.3) so white and light prints keep their detail.
- **Pro mode** (if your phone has it and you're comfortable): ISO 50–100, white balance "daylight" (about 5200K), focus on the product. Otherwise auto is fine with the lock above.
- **Clean the lens** with a soft cloth before every session.

## 4. Every product: 3 shots on the grey/beige sweep

1. **Hero:** front, slight 3/4 angle, whole object, centred, plenty of empty space around it.
2. **Detail:** close-up of what makes it special (paint, texture, joint, engraving).
3. **Scale:** next to a LKR 10 coin, or in a clean hand, on the same background.

**Rules (from NEXT-PHASE §1):** one product per photo, no other products in the frame, no hands in the hero shot, no filters, nothing AI-generated or edited to look different from the real print.

## 5. The hero shot on black (website front page)

For the website's main picture and your Google cover photo.

- **Product:** Night Fury Keychain, or Kurama Figure (the orange stands out on black).
- **Background:** black velvet as a sweep. **Lint-roll it** right before shooting; every speck shows on black.
- **Light:** the window from **one side only**. **No** white fill card, so you get deep shadows and a bright edge on the product.
- **Exposure:** tap on the product, lock, then slide exposure **down** (−0.7 to −1.0) until the background is pure black and the product still looks bright.
- **Framing:** take two versions:
  - **Landscape** (phone sideways) with the product on the **right third** and empty black on the left (the website puts text there).
  - **Portrait** (phone upright), product slightly low in the frame.
- Take 10+ frames, moving the product a few degrees each time; you'll pick the best one later.

## 6. After the shoot

1. Pick the best 3 per product (plus the black hero shots).
2. **No edits** except crop and straighten. Leave brightness alone if you locked exposure properly.
3. Name the files with the product ID from the website, e.g. `HC-KURAMA-hero.jpg`, `HC-KURAMA-detail.jpg`, `HC-KURAMA-scale.jpg`, `HNYCMB501-black-landscape.jpg`.
4. Upload the **originals** (not WhatsApp copies: WhatsApp shrinks them) to the product's Drive folder.
5. Tell Claude **"new photos are in Drive for …"**. The PM session imports them into the website with the usual hash-checked steps, and Claude updates the 30-day plan to use them.

## 8. Quick checks before you post

- Is the background the same colour as your other photos?
- Is the product sharp where it matters (the face, the emblem)?
- Is anything else in the frame (cables, dust, a second product)?
- Does it look like the real thing a customer will receive? If not, reshoot.
