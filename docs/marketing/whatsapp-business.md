# WhatsApp Business setup (071 059 9815)

WhatsApp is where every order arrives, so it's worth 30 minutes to make it look as tidy as the website. Everything here is done in the **WhatsApp Business app** on the phone with **071 059 9815**. Nothing has been changed; you do these steps.

Menu names below are from the Android app; on iPhone they're under **Settings → Business tools**. Wording can differ a little between app versions.

## 1. Business profile

**⋮ → Settings → Business tools → Business profile**

| Field | What to put |
|---|---|
| Photo | The hexagon-and-bee symbol on studio black (the same one as Instagram; say "export the profile picture") |
| Name | `HoneyComb 3D` |
| Category | **Shopping & retail** (or Arts & entertainment) |
| Description | see below |
| Address | **Leave empty.** You don't need to show your home. |
| Business hours | `{the hours you answer}` (e.g. Mon–Sat 9 am – 7 pm). Choose them; they drive the away message. |
| Email | The new email from `launch-kit.md` |
| Website 1 | `https://honeycomb3d.store` |
| Website 2 | Your Instagram link, once it exists |

**Description** (WhatsApp allows up to 512 characters):
```
3D-printed anime, film and comic figures, keychains and props, made to order in Kegalle, Sri Lanka 🐝
Browse the designs at honeycomb3d.store and tap Order now, or send us a photo or link of what you'd like.
We confirm the size, colour, price and delivery with you before we print.
```

**About** (the short line under your name): `3D prints made to order · honeycomb3d.store`

## 3. Labels

**Chats → ⋮ → Labels.** WhatsApp comes with a few labels; rename or add these so every order chat shows its stage at a glance. Long-press a chat → label icon to apply one.

| Label | Colour (suggestion) | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| New order | yellow | An order request arrives (from the website or the catalog) |
| Quoted | orange | You've sent the total and are waiting for a yes |
| Paid | green | Payment received |
| Printing | blue | It's on the printer or being finished |
| Shipped | purple | Handed to the courier or delivered |
| Custom request | grey | Someone asks for a design that isn't on the site |

Move the chat to the next label at each step. Remove "New order" once you've replied.

```
honeycomb3d.store එකෙන් ඕඩර් එකක් එව්වා නම්, අපි ඒක බලලා ඉක්මනින්ම details එක්ක reply කරනවා.
```

## 5. Away message

**Business tools → Away message → On → Schedule: Outside of business hours** (uses the hours from step 1).

```

Your order request is saved here, so there's no need to send it again.

ඔයාගේ message එකට ස්තුතියි 🐝 අපි දැන් ළඟ නෑ, ආපහු ආවම reply කරනවා.
```

Replace `{next working day / tomorrow morning}` with what's true for your hours, or delete that part.

## 6. Quick replies

**Business tools → Quick replies → +.** Type `/` in a chat to pick one. Each shortcut below uses the text from `order-replies.md` (English and Sinhala are separate quick replies, e.g. `/confirm` and `/confirmsi`).

| Shortcut | Template in `order-replies.md` |
|---|---|
| `/confirm` · `/confirmsi` | 1. Confirm the design and colour |
| `/total` · `/totalsi` | 2. The total |
| `/address` · `/addresssi` | 3. Ask for the delivery address |
| `/pay` · `/paysi` | 4. Payment instructions (**only after you've filled in the placeholders**) |
| `/sent` · `/sentsi` | 5. Order dispatched |
| `/thanks` · `/thankssi` | 6. Thank you + review request |
| `/custom` · `/customsi` | 7. Custom request reply |

Quick replies keep the `{placeholders}`: fill them in each time before you press send. **Never send a message with `{...}` still in it.**

## 7. Safety

- Turn on **Settings → Account → Two-step verification** with a PIN only you know.
- Only you use this number. Don't add the old business numbers anywhere, and don't forward chats from them.
- Back up chats (Settings → Chats → Chat backup) to your own Google account.
