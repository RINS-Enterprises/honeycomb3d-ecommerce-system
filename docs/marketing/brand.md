# HoneyComb 3D brand guide (one page)

Taken from the live site, honeycomb3d.store: the colours, font and words below are the ones the site already uses. Use this page whenever you make a post, a story, a sticker or a price card.

## The look in one line

**A dark studio with one honey-coloured light.** Warm near-black backgrounds, the print as the star, honey orange used sparingly for the one thing you want people to notice.

## Colours

| Name | Hex | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Studio black | `#0C0B0A` | Main background of every post and story |
| Panel | `#191613` | Cards and boxes on top of the black |
| Raised panel | `#2A251F` | A second layer, e.g. a price tag box |
| Warm white | `#F6F1E8` | Headings and main text |
| Soft text | `#B4AB9D` | Small print, the website line, captions on images |
| **Honey** | `#FBA31B` | Accent only: one word, a button, a price, the logo hexagon |
| Honey light | `#FFD391` | Rarely: a highlight on honey |
| Text on honey | `#16110A` | Text placed on a honey box |

Rule of thumb: **about 90% dark, 10% honey.** If a post looks orange overall, there is too much honey.

## Fonts

- **Headings: Bricolage Grotesque**, SemiBold (600). Free (SIL Open Font License) and on Google Fonts. Tight letter spacing, short lines.
  - In Canva: search "Bricolage Grotesque" in the font list. If it is not there, use **Inter Tight SemiBold** instead and tell Claude, so the guide matches what you use.
- **Body text:** any clean sans-serif (the phone's system font on the site). In Canva use **Inter** or **Noto Sans**.
- **Sinhala text:** **Noto Sans Sinhala** (free, on Google Fonts and in Canva). Don't stretch or outline Sinhala letters.
- **Small labels** ("THE COLLECTION", "MADE TO ORDER"): capitals, small, honey colour, wide letter spacing. That's the site's "eyebrow" style.

## Logo

Files: `public/assets/honeycomb-logo-dark-660.png` (large) and `public/assets/honeycomb-logo-dark-264.png` (small). The original is `assets/brand-sources/honeycomb-logo-black.pdf`.

The logo is the honey hexagon with the bee, plus a **white** wordmark. It's made for dark backgrounds.

**Do**
- Put it on studio black or a dark photo area.
- Leave clear space around it at least as wide as the bee's head.
- For a profile picture (a small circle), use the **hexagon and bee only**, centred on studio black. Crop it from the 660 px file; don't redraw it.

**Don't**
- Put it on white or a light photo. The white wordmark disappears. (A light-background version does not exist yet; ask Claude to prepare one from the original PDF if you need it.)
- Recolour, stretch, rotate, add shadows or outlines, or put it inside another shape.
- Use the old logo files or graphics from the old accounts.
- Make a new logo with an AI tool.

## Photos

- One product per photo, on a plain background. The **hero look is a product on black** (see `photo-guide.md`).
- Real photos of real prints only. No AI images, no stock photos, no renders presented as the printed object.
- No filters. Light edits only: crop, straighten, brightness.

## Tone of voice

The site already speaks like this. Keep it the same everywhere.

- **Friendly and plain.** Short sentences. "Pick a design, send your request on WhatsApp, and we confirm the colour, price and delivery with you."
- **Honest about made to order.** Everything is printed after you order; size, colour and finish are confirmed in the chat.
- **Fan to fan.** Name the character and the series; show you care about the detail.
- **"We"** for the business, **"you"** for the customer.
- **Lines from the site you can reuse:** "Small objects. Big character." · "3D-printed collectibles, made in Sri Lanka." · "Can't find it? Let's talk about making it." · "From favourite to yours."

### Do / don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Kurama Figure, LKR 1,500. Printed to order in Kegalle. Message us on WhatsApp: 071 059 9815." | "Best quality in Sri Lanka!!! 🔥🔥🔥" (an untested claim) |
| "Size and colour are confirmed with you on WhatsApp." | "Delivered in 2 days islandwide." (not decided yet) |
| "Ask us about colours." | "Only 3 left!" (made to order, so there is no stock to run out) |
| "Which one should we print next? Vote in the comments." | "50% off today only" (no discounts have been set) |
| Share real customer photos, with their OK. | Write reviews yourself or ask friends to post fake ones. |
| Use the exact price from the website. | Round or change prices in a post. |

The figures are of well-known characters (Naruto, Marvel, Star Wars, How to Train Your Dragon and others). In posts, say "inspired by" or name the character, don't use studio logos or official artwork, and never call the prints "official" or "licensed".
