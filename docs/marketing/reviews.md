# Customer reviews: asking for real ones

**The one rule: only real reviews from real customers.** Never write a review yourself, never ask friends or family to review something they didn't buy, and never offer a discount or gift in exchange for a review. Google removes such reviews and can suspend the profile; customers can tell, too.

## Who to ask

- Customers who have **received** their order, about **3–7 days after** it arrived (enough time to enjoy it).
- Ask **once**. If they don't reply, don't chase.
- Start with your happiest recent customers in your WhatsApp chats.

## The WhatsApp message

Send it from **071 059 9815**, in the customer's existing chat. Fill in the [brackets]. Use version A until your Google profile is live (`google-business-profile.md`), then version B.

If you're happy for us to share it on our Instagram, just say so. We'll only use your first name, or no name if you prefer.
Thank you for ordering from us!

Notes:
- Ask for an **honest** review, not a 5-star one. Don't write "please give us 5 stars".
- Get the **[Google review link]** from your profile: **Ask for reviews** → copy link.
- If a customer had a problem, **fix the problem first** and don't send the review request until they're happy (and even then, it's their choice what they write).

## Where real reviews go

| Where | How | Permission needed |
|---|---|---|
| **Google profile** | Customers post them directly with the link above. Reply to each one within a few days: thank them by first name, keep it short. Reply calmly to any bad review and offer to fix it on WhatsApp. | They post it themselves. |
| **Instagram "Reviews" highlight** | Screenshot their WhatsApp message or photo → post to your story → add to the **Reviews** highlight (cover in `launch-kit.md`). **Crop out phone numbers and profile pictures.** Show first name only, or none, as they chose. | **Yes**, a clear "yes, you can share it" in the chat. |
| **Instagram feed** | Customer photo posts: "From [first name] in [town, only if they're OK with it]: [their words]". Credit them. | **Yes**, for both the photo and the words. |
| **Website (later)** | Once you have several real reviews, the PM can add a reviews section to honeycomb3d.store using only the exact words customers sent, with their permission. Claude never writes or edits review text. | **Yes**, and keep the screenshot of their permission. |

## Keep a simple log

A note on your phone or a sheet with: date · first name · product · review text or "photo" · **permission yes/no** · where used (Google / Instagram / site). If someone later asks you to take it down, remove it the same day.

## Never

- Write, edit or "improve" a customer's words.
- Post a review on the old Kandy listing or any old account.
- Use a customer's photo, name or words without their OK.
- Use stock photos or AI images as "customer photos".
- Give discounts, freebies or contest entries for reviews.
