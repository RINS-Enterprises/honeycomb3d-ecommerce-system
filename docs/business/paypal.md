# PayPal: getting paid from overseas

For HoneyComb 3D: overseas buyers, such as Sri Lankans abroad buying a gift delivered here, paying in USD. Research date: **19 Oct 2026**. Nothing was signed up for or logged into.

**How to read this:** ✅ official (PayPal's or the bank's own page) · 📰 news, with date · ❓ not published, ask.

---

## Quick answer

- **Yes, you can receive PayPal now, but only from abroad.** Since **15 May 2026**, a Sri Lankan **personal** PayPal account can receive payments **from other countries** once it is linked to **Commercial Bank** or **Sampath Bank** ✅. A customer paying from a Sri Lankan PayPal account **cannot** pay you.
- **How you ask for money:** use PayPal's **"Request money"**: the buyer's email address plus a USD amount. PayPal emails the buyer a "Pay" button. Invoices, website checkout and business accounts are still **"coming soon"** ✅.
- **Cost:** on a USD 50 order you keep about **USD 43** after PayPal's fee and the ComBank withdrawal fee (our arithmetic below). The loss is much higher on small orders, so **withdraw in batches**.
- The website's PayPal option (`paypalOverseas`) is **built but switched off**. Switch it on only after the test payment below works.

## Step 1: Link PayPal in your bank app

You need: age 18+, a savings or current account at the bank, and the **same email and date of birth** on PayPal and at the bank (Sampath also checks your **mobile number**). The PayPal account must be **personal, verified, and registered in Sri Lanka**.

### Commercial Bank: ComBank Digital app ✅ ([ComBank PayPal page](https://www.combank.lk/services/paypal), [FAQ](https://www.combank.lk/info/215))
1. Open **ComBank Digital** (mobile app only).
2. Go to **View all my accounts** and find **PayPal**. Tap **Connect**.
3. Agree to share your email address.
4. Log in to PayPal, **or** create a new PayPal account (verified with an OTP).
5. Tap **Agree and Connect**.

ComBank pays out to an **LKR account only**; foreign-currency accounts can't be used.

### Sampath Bank: Sampath Vishwa app ✅ ([Sampath PayPal page](https://www.sampath.lk/digital-banking/online-banking?section=Sampath-PayPal), [press release, 26 May 2026](https://www.sampath.lk/news-and-media/press-releases/Sampath-Bank-launches-PayPal-integration-to-expand-global-payment-access-for-Sri-Lankans))
1. Open **Sampath Vishwa**.
2. Under **Quick Actions**, tap **PayPal**.
3. Create a new PayPal account or link your existing one.

Sampath can pay out to an **LKR or a USD** Sampath account.

## Step 2: The test payment

1. Ask a friend or relative **abroad** to pay you. Send them a **Request money** (see Step 3).
2. Check the payment shows in PayPal as **received**, not "pending" or "refused".
3. Then try a **withdrawal** in the bank app.

**About the USD 1 test:** USD 1 is enough to prove that **receiving works**. After PayPal's fee you would hold about **USD 0.66**, and ComBank's **minimum withdrawal fee is USD 3.50**, so you probably **can't withdraw** a USD 1 test at ComBank (our reading of the fee). Two options:
- **Test with USD 1** to prove receiving, and leave it in PayPal to withdraw later with real orders; **or**
- **Test with USD 10** to prove receiving **and** withdrawal. You'd keep about USD 5.76 of it (our arithmetic).

When it works, tell Claude: **"PayPal works, switch it on."**

---

## Step 3: Asking a customer to pay (each order)

1. **Agree the order and the USD total in WhatsApp**, including delivery. Get the buyer's **PayPal email address**.
2. In the PayPal app: **Send and Request → Request**. Enter the buyer's email and the **USD amount**. In the note, write the order, e.g. *"HoneyComb 3D order: Kurama figure ×1, delivery to Colombo 05."*
3. PayPal emails the buyer a link to pay. Tell them in WhatsApp: *"I've sent a PayPal request to your email."*
4. **Wait until the payment shows as received in PayPal before printing.**
5. Ship it **with tracking**, to the address written in the PayPal payment (see protection below).

PayPal treats these requests as **goods and services** ✅, which is what you want for seller protection.

## Fees

### PayPal ✅ ([merchant fees, updated 28 Sep 2026](https://www.paypal.com/lk/webapps/mpp/merchant-fees))
- **Receiving** an international goods-and-services payment: **4.40% + USD 0.30**.
- **Currency conversion** in PayPal: **3.0%** above the base rate. Whether this also applies when the bank converts your withdrawal isn't stated ❓.
- Dispute fee **USD 8**; chargeback fee **USD 10**.

### Withdrawing to your bank
- **Commercial Bank** ✅ ([FAQ](https://www.combank.lk/info/215)): **3% + USD 3.00** per withdrawal, **minimum USD 3.50**. Instant, and converted to LKR at ComBank's daily rate.
- **Sampath:** **not published** ❓. Its tariff (updated 4 Jun 2026) has no PayPal line. The app shows the fee before you confirm.

### What you keep (our arithmetic, ComBank, one withdrawal per order)

| Order | After PayPal fee | After ComBank withdrawal | Lost |
|---|---|---|---|
| USD 10 | USD 9.26 | USD 5.76 | 42% |
| USD 20 | USD 18.82 | USD 15.26 | 24% |
| USD 50 | USD 47.50 | USD 43.08 | 14% |
| USD 100 | USD 95.30 | USD 89.44 | 11% |

**What this means:**
- **Withdraw in batches.** The USD 3 part of the bank fee is per withdrawal, so one withdrawal of USD 100 costs much less than five of USD 20.
- **Set a minimum PayPal order**, e.g. **USD 20**, and price USD quotes about **10–15% above** the LKR price.
- Bank exchange rates also vary. Compare with the Central Bank rate on the day.

## Protect yourself ✅ ([PayPal seller protection, updated 26 Jan 2026](https://www.paypal.com/lk/legalhub/paypal/seller-protection?locale.x=en_LK))
- **Never accept "Friends and Family" for a sale.** Personal payments are **not covered** by seller protection.
- Seller protection covers **"unauthorised payment"** and **"item not received"** claims, **if** you ship to the **address on the PayPal payment**, use **online tracking**, and the tracking shows **"delivered"**. SL Post Courier has tracking (see [delivery.md](delivery.md)).
- It does **not** cover "**not as described**" claims. For custom prints, keep the WhatsApp messages agreeing the design, size and colour, and a photo of the finished item before posting.
- For a gift going to someone else in Sri Lanka, ask the buyer to put the **recipient's address** in the payment, or note it in WhatsApp. Protection needs the address on the payment.
