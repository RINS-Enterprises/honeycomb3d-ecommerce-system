## 1. Put the partnership in writing (first, before money grows)
Isuru and Nipuna should agree one page and both sign it. Profit was not shared before, and that is exactly what a written agreement prevents. Cover:
1. **What each person owns:** printers, filament stock, the brand name, the domain honeycomb3d.store, and the WhatsApp, Instagram and Airtable accounts.
2. **Money:** who pays costs, the profit split (for example 50/50 after costs), when it's paid (for example monthly), and where the money sits (one business bank account once registered).
3. **Spending:** what anyone can spend alone (for example up to LKR 10,000), and what needs both of you.
4. **Helpers:** how team members are paid (per piece, per day, or a share).
5. **If someone leaves:** who keeps what, and how the other is paid out.

A template like this is a starting point. For anything large (registration as a partnership, investment), a lawyer or notary should review it.

## 3. A system that works with ADHD, not against it
- **One board, one place:** an Airtable board (the base already exists) with columns *To print → Printing → Painting → Packing → Shipped*. Every order and every task is a card with **one owner and one due date**. If it isn't on the board, it doesn't exist.
- **20-minute Sunday check-in** (same time every week; set a phone alarm):
  1. what shipped
  2. what's stuck
  3. each person names **one** priority for the week
- **Checklists beat memory.** Print a one-page checklist and stick it by the printers: *order confirmed → printed → cleaned → painted → photo → packed → posted → customer messaged*.
- **Let tools remind you.** The Make automations (Session A's recipes) send the reminders and the weekly summary, so nobody has to remember to check.
- **Batch the boring work.** One afternoon a week for packing and posting, one for photos. Fewer switches between tasks means less gets dropped.
- **Finish before starting.** No new product idea goes on the board until the current week's orders are shipped.

## 4. Product ladder and pricing
| Level | What it is | How it's priced |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Standard print | Current shop items, single colour or multi-colour | Current prices |
| 2. Painted premium | Hand-painted by Jayani, photographed properly | Base price + painting time + a premium for the finish |
| 3. Display edition | Painted figure in a display box or case, with a name plate | Painted price + case cost + presentation premium |

**Price every product from cost, not guesswork.** A simple sheet (one row per product):
- filament grams × cost per gram
- print hours × a machine rate (electricity + wear)
- paint minutes × a painter rate
- packaging
- a margin of at least 40–50% on top

Session C's guides cover the gateway fees to add on top.

**Resin printer:** buy it when painted-line orders show demand. Write the trigger down now, for example "after 20 painted orders in a month, or LKR X saved in the business account". That way it's a plan, not an impulse.
