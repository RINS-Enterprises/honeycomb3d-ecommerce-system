# Delivery: couriers from Kegalle

For HoneyComb 3D (small figures and keychains, usually under 500 g). Research date: **19 Oct 2026**. Nobody was contacted and nothing was signed up for.

**How to read this:**
- ✅ **Official**: from the courier's own website or documents.
- ⚠️ **Third-party**: a blog or news site, or a search-engine summary because the site would not load from here. Check before you rely on it.
- ❓ **Ask**: the courier publishes nothing. Use the phone script below.

---

## Quick answer

- **Only Sri Lanka Post publishes a full price list.** It is also the cheapest, and anyone can send COD at the counter without a registered business.
- **Domex, Koombiyo, Pronto and Prompt Xpress have branches in Kegalle town.** SONIC's head office is in Mawanella. Their prices are not published (Domex only via an old third-party table), so call them.
- **Recommended delivery line (owner to confirm):**
  > Islandwide delivery by courier, **LKR 400**. Usually **2–4 working days** after your order is printed.

  Why: SL Post Courier costs Rs 200 (≤250 g), Rs 250 (≤500 g) or Rs 350 (≤1 kg). LKR 400 covers that plus the box and bubble wrap. If you pick a private courier after calling, LKR 500 is safer (see "Recommendation" at the end).

## Each courier in short

### Sri Lanka Post ✅
- **Prices:** the official schedule effective **9 Feb 2026** ([PDF](https://slpost.gov.lk/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/domesticpostagerevision2026-02-09.pdf), linked from [slpost.gov.lk/tariff](https://slpost.gov.lk/tariff)). Courier and parcel prices were **unchanged** in that revision: they match the [2023 courier price image](https://slpost.gov.lk/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/slpostcourier_charges.jpg), and NewsFirst and Ada Derana reported the revision on 9 Feb 2026.
- **SL Post Courier:** the same price everywhere in the island. The [service page](https://slpost.gov.lk/services/speed-post-courier-tracking/) says delivery is "24 hours" within your zone and "48 hours" outside it, and that dates may vary. Tracking is included.
- **Ordinary parcel:** cheaper, no COD, delivery time not stated. Registration is listed at Rs 60. Ask at the counter whether a "registered parcel" costs the parcel price plus Rs 60.
- **COD:** hand it in at any post office counter. **No registered business is needed.** The optional online account needs a BR certificate only if you open it in a business name. The maximum value is Rs 100,000. If the item can't be delivered, it is kept for 5 days and then returned to you. After delivery you get a payment notice and collect the cash at any post office. No fixed number of days is stated. Sources: [COD page](https://slpost.gov.lk/cash-on-delivery-service/) and [COD manual, Oct 2023](https://slpost.gov.lk/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/SL-Post-COD-English-New.pdf).
- **Pickup:** none mentioned. You drop the parcel at the post office.
- "EMS" on slpost.gov.lk is **international only**. For local express, use SL Post Courier.

### Domex
- **Prices ⚠️:** Rs 350 (≤250 g), about Rs 430 (≤500 g), Rs 510 (≤1 kg), Rs 590 (1–2 kg). This comes from a third-party table, "Delivery Charges 2025" (studentpublication.lk), which is now **offline**: the figures are from search summaries. Domex itself has only a quote tool ([apps.domexweb.com](https://apps.domexweb.com/)).
- **COD ✅:** "cash settlement every 15 days". The fee is described only as "affordable handling charges", so ask ([services](https://domex.lk/services.php)).
- **Kegalle ✅:** No 41/1 Kumarathunga Munidasa Mw., Kegalle. Also a branch in Mawanella ([branches](https://domex.lk/branches.php)).
- **Days ✅:** next-day ("overnight") delivery. Saturday delivery is available.
- **Account:** "Cash/COD", "Corporate" and "E-Commerce" types. Whether a registered business is needed is not stated, so ask.

### SONIC Express ⚠️
- The website ([sonicexpress.lk/pricing](https://sonicexpress.lk/pricing)) **did not load** (expired security certificate). These figures come from search summaries.
- **Prices:** Rs 350 within the district and Rs 500 to other districts, for the first 1 kg. Volumetric weight (L×W×H cm ÷ 5000) counts if it is higher than the real weight.
- **COD:** "1.5%, minimum Rs 100" appeared in only one summary. Unverified.
- **Where:** head office on Main Street, Mawanella. Easy to visit and confirm in person.

### Koombiyo Delivery
- **Prices:** not published. The site claims the "lowest rate ever".
- **COD ✅:** offered, with a "one week" cash cycle ([koombiyodelivery.lk](https://koombiyodelivery.lk/)).
- **Kegalle ✅:** branch on Winston Wickramasinghe Mawatha, Kegalle, tel 035 222 5161. Also a branch in Mawanella ([branch map](https://koombiyodelivery.lk/branchmap)).
- **Days:** not stated. It makes three delivery attempts on consecutive days.

### Pronto Lanka
- **Prices and COD fee:** not published. COD is offered.
- **Kegalle ✅:** 249 Anguruwella, Colombo Rd, Kegalle, tel 035 203 1020. "Picked up from the doorstep free of charge" ([prontolanka.lk](https://prontolanka.lk/)).

### Prompt Xpress
- **Prices and COD:** not published. COD is not mentioned.
- **Kegalle ✅:** branch tel 035 493 5210. Also a branch in Mawanella ([branch network](https://promptxpress.lk/BranchNetwork.aspx)).
- **Days ✅:** "within 24 hours anywhere of the island".

### Trans Express
- **Prices and COD fee:** not published.
- **COD payout ✅:** "weekly or bi-weekly" ([services](https://transexpress.lk/services/our-services/)), but the homepage says merchants are paid "365 days a year". Ask which applies.
- **Kegalle:** only the Colombo-area head office is listed. A "Courier on Call" pickup request exists ([page](https://transexpress.lk/service/courier-on-call/)). Ask whether they pick up in Kegalle.
- **Days ✅:** next-day ("send today, arrive tomorrow").

### Citypak
- **Prices:** calculator only.
- **COD ✅:** "minimal handling fee". Settlement is **daily** (next working day) or **weekly** (paid Wednesday) ([FAQ](https://citypak.lk/faq/)).
- **Days ✅:** 1–3 business days to customers.
- **Kegalle:** no branch found among its "45+ branches". You can book a pickup by hotline or WhatsApp.
- **Account:** the client panel is "for businesses". Ask whether an individual can sign up.

### Grasshoppers (Kapruka group)
- The website blocked this research tool. A search summary of its own About page says COD with "guaranteed weekly settlements".
- **Prices, COD fee and Kegalle coverage:** unknown. Its online-shop plugin needs a "corporate ID" from them, which suggests an approved account.

## Phone script (about 3 minutes per courier)

Call **three**: **Domex Kegalle**, **Koombiyo Kegalle** and **Pronto Kegalle**. Optionally SONIC in Mawanella. Have the quote sheet below open.

> **Hello, my name is Isuru. I run a small online shop in Kegalle selling 3D-printed figures and keychains. I'd like to send about ___ parcels a week, islandwide. Could you help me with your rates?**
>
> 1. What is the price for one parcel to another district: **up to 250 g, up to 500 g and up to 1 kg?** Is VAT included?
> 2. Is it cheaper **inside Kegalle District**?
> 3. Do you offer **cash on delivery**? What is the **COD fee**: a fixed amount or a percentage? Is there a minimum?
> 4. **How often do you pay me** the COD money, and how: bank transfer or cash?
> 5. Will you **pick up from my home in Kegalle**? Is there a charge? Is there a minimum number of parcels?
> 6. How many **working days** does delivery usually take to Colombo? To Jaffna or Batticaloa?
> 7. Can I open an account as an **individual, without a business registration**? What documents do you need?
> 8. If the customer **refuses or isn't home**, what is the return charge?
> 9. What happens if a parcel is **damaged or lost**? Is there any cover?
>
> **Thank you. Can you send these rates to me on WhatsApp at 071 059 9815?**

Tips: write the person's name and the date. Ask for prices **in writing** (WhatsApp is fine). Don't sign or pay anything on the call.

## Recommendation (for you to confirm)

1. **Start with SL Post Courier.** It is the cheapest published option, there's a post office in Kegalle, it tracks parcels, and it allows COD **without a registered business**.
2. **Call Domex, Koombiyo and Pronto** with the script. A private courier is worth it if it offers **home pickup** and **weekly bank payouts** at a similar price.
3. **Delivery line for the website:**
   - With SL Post Courier: **"Islandwide delivery by courier, LKR 400. Usually 2–4 working days after your order is printed."**
   - If you choose a private courier (about Rs 350–510 per parcel, plus any COD fee): use **LKR 500** instead.
4. **"2–4 working days"** is deliberately longer than SL Post's stated "24–48 hours". It leaves room for packing, posting days and far districts. Shorten it once you see real delivery times.
5. **COD:** if you offer it, say **"Cash on delivery available"**. Don't add a COD surcharge until you know the courier's fee. On SL Post it's about Rs 80 on a Rs 1,500 order (the Rs 50 service charge plus Rs 30 money-order fee).

**Reply to Claude with one line**, for example:
> "Delivery: islandwide by courier, LKR 400, usually 2–4 working days after printing."
