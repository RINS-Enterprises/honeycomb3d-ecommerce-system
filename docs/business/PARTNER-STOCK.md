# Partner stock: products printed by the partner, sold by HoneyComb 3D

Recorded 2026-10-31 from the owner (Isuru).

## The arrangement, in the owner's words
- **The partner** prints the products. **HoneyComb 3D** (Isuru) lists and sells them on the website and in person.
- HoneyComb sets the selling price ("we can increase the price as much as we want") and keeps a **commission of 50% of the profit** on each sale. The partner keeps the other 50%.
- Profit is not otherwise shared between the partners at the moment. This commission covers only items HoneyComb sells.

## Still to agree (with the partner, in writing)
1. **What "profit" means:** selling price minus what, exactly? For example the partner's material and machine cost, or an agreed cost price per item. Write one cost price per item before listing it.
2. **Who pays delivery, packaging and payment-gateway fees**, and whether they come off before the profit split.
3. **When and how the partner is paid** (e.g. weekly, by bank transfer), plus a simple sales log both partners can see.
4. **Returns and damaged items:** whose cost is a reprint?

The partnership-agreement template in `OPERATING-PLAN.md` §1 covers these points. Use it.
