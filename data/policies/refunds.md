Most of our products are made to order for you. This policy explains when you can cancel and what we do if something is wrong with your order. It is part of our [Terms and conditions](/terms/).

## Cancelling an order
- **Before we start printing:** you can cancel for a **full refund**. We tell you on WhatsApp when printing starts. Message us on WhatsApp at {{phone}} or email {{email}}.
- **After printing has started:** we can't cancel, because the item is being made for you. If you change your mind at this point, please talk to us and we will try to help.

## Damaged, faulty or wrong items
If your order arrives **damaged, faulty or different from what you ordered**, we will make it right:

1. Contact us within **48 hours** of receiving your order, on WhatsApp at {{phone}} or by email at {{email}}.
2. Send a photo of the item and of the packaging, plus your order details.
3. We check the photos and reply. If the problem is confirmed, you choose a **free reprint and redelivery** or a **full refund** of the item and its delivery charge.

We may ask you to send the item back so we can check it. If so, we pay the return postage.

## Change of mind
Because our products are printed to order, we **do not accept returns or give refunds for a change of mind**. This includes choosing the wrong design, colour or size. Please check the details in your quote before you confirm your order. This does not affect your rights under Sri Lankan consumer law for items that are faulty or not as described.

## What is not a fault
These are part of how 3D printing and hand-painting work, so they are not faults:
- small differences in colour or finish
- visible print layer lines
- small variations between hand-painted pieces

Damage caused after delivery, for example by drops, heat or misuse, is also not covered.

## How refunds are paid
- We refund using the **same method you paid with** where possible: bank transfer, or a reversal to your card through our payment partner.
- We send approved refunds within **7 working days**.
- Card refunds can take extra time to appear, depending on your bank.
- If you paid cash on delivery, we refund by bank transfer to an account you give us.
