This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and how we protect it. We follow Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022.

## Who is responsible
{{seller}}, contactable at {{email}} or on WhatsApp at {{phone}}. We are responsible for your personal data.

## What this website stores
- **No accounts:** the site has no account system and no analytics tracking.
- **On your device only:** the request cart keeps product IDs and quantities in your browser's local storage, with a short list of designs you viewed recently. It stays on your device. Remove the items, use "Clear" under Recently viewed, or clear your browser's site data to delete it.
- **Not saved:** cart notes and custom-enquiry fields are not saved by the site.
- **Hosting logs:** our hosting provider processes ordinary technical logs, such as IP address and pages requested, to run and secure the site.

## What you share with us when you order
When you send an order request or contact us, we receive:
- your name, phone number and the messages you send us on WhatsApp
- your delivery address, when you give it
- your order details and payment confirmation (for example a bank transfer reference)
- photos you send us, for example of a damaged item

Opening WhatsApp from this site puts your prepared message into a WhatsApp link. You review it in WhatsApp and choose whether to send it. WhatsApp handles that conversation under its own terms and privacy policy.

## Why we use it
We use your data only to:
- confirm, make, deliver and support your order
- take payment and give refunds
- answer your questions
- keep the records the law requires, such as sales records

We do not sell your personal data. We do not send you marketing messages unless you ask us to.

## Who we share it with
Only with the people who help us complete your order:
- **Couriers or the postal service:** your name, address and phone number, to deliver your parcel.
- **Our payment partner:** for card payments. They process your card details on their own secure page. **We never see or store your full card number.**
- **Authorities:** where the law requires it.

## How long we keep it
We keep order records for as long as the law requires for business and tax records, and other messages and photos only as long as needed to complete and support your order.

## Your rights
You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or to stop using it, unless the law requires us to keep it. Message us on WhatsApp at {{phone}} or email {{email}}. We will reply within 14 days.

## Security
We keep order information in access-controlled accounts and share it only as described here. This website uses HTTPS.

## Changes
We will update this page if the way we handle data changes.
