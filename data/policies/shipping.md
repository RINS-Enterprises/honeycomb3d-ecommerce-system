We send orders from Kegalle to addresses across Sri Lanka. This policy is part of our [Terms and conditions](/terms/).

## Where we deliver
We deliver **islandwide in Sri Lanka** by courier or registered post. For orders outside Sri Lanka, please contact us first. We will tell you if we can send them and what it costs.

## Delivery cost and time
{{delivery}}

Made-to-order items are printed after your order is confirmed. The **printing time** depends on the design and quantity, and we tell you the estimate in your quote. The **delivery time** starts when we hand your parcel to the courier.

## Tracking
When your order is sent, we message you on WhatsApp with the courier name and, where the courier gives one, a tracking number.

## Your address and delivery
- Please give a full address and a phone number the courier can reach.
- If a parcel comes back to us because the address was wrong or nobody could be reached, we will contact you. The cost of sending it again may be charged.

## Damage in transit
We pack every order carefully. If your parcel arrives damaged, keep the packaging and contact us within **48 hours** with photos. Our [Refund, return and cancellation policy](/refunds/) explains what we do.

## Collection
If you are near Kegalle, you can ask us about collecting your order in person. We will agree a time with you on WhatsApp.
